$$g'(0)=\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{g(x)-g(0)}{x-0}=\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{x^3f(x)+\cancel{e^2}-\cancel{e^2}}{x-0}=\lim_{x\to 0}x^2f(x)=0^2f(0)=0.$$

$$\lim_{x\to 0} f(x)=f(0),$$
